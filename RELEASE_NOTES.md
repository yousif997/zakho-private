**Zakho Private Records 1.1.0**

- Doctor and secretary computers: the secretary registers patients and sends them to the doctor with the complaint, vital signs and diagnoses.
- Waiting list on the doctor's screen, with a sound and taskbar alert when a patient is sent.
- Automatic updates: the program checks for a new version and installs it on the next restart.

Install: download `Zakho-Private-Records-Setup-1.1.0.exe` below and run it. Existing patient records are kept.
